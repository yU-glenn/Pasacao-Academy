Program

Elementary Education

Our Elementary program provides a strong academic foundation through a comprehensive curriculum that fosters holistic child development. Students are taught critical thinking, creativity, and character formation.

The curriculum covers core subjects including English, Mathematics, Science, Filipino, Araling Panlipunan, MAPEH, and Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao, along with enrichment programs in technology, arts, and sports.
