Admissions Process

Your future begins here. Follow our simple admissions process to join PASACAO ACADEMY.

1. Submit Application

Complete application form and submit all required documents.

2. Entrance Exam

Take the university entrance examination to assess your readiness.

3. Interview

Attend an interview with our admissions panel.

4. Enrollment

Receive your acceptance letter and complete enrollment.

Requirements

Admission Requirements

Freshmen

Form 138 / Report Card, PSA Birth Certificate, Good Moral Certificate, 2x2 ID Photos, NCAE Result

Transferees

Transcript of Records, Honorable Dismissal, PSA Birth Certificate, Good Moral Certificate

Financial Aid

Scholarships & Financial Aid

We offer various scholarship programs to support deserving students.

Academic Scholarship

Full or partial tuition coverage for students with outstanding academic performance.

Athletic Scholarship

For student-athletes who excel in sports and represent the university in competitions.

Financial Assistance

Need-based grants and payment plans to ensure access to quality education.
