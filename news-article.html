Annual Graduation Ceremony 2025

June 15, 2025

PASACAO ACADEMY celebrated its 65th Annual Foundation Ceremony at the Grand Sports Complex on March, 2026. The event was attended by thousands of graduates, their families, faculty members, and distinguished guests.

Dr. Maria Santos, President of PASACAO ACADEMY, delivered an inspiring commencement address, congratulating the graduates for their hard work and dedication. "You are now prepared to face the challenges of the future with confidence and integrity," she said.

The ceremony featured the conferment of degrees to over 2,000 graduates from various colleges and programs. Special awards were given to outstanding students who excelled academically and in extracurricular activities.

The guest speaker, Juan dela Cruz (CEO of Tech Innovations Inc. and an alumnus of PASACAO ACADEMY), shared his journey from student to successful entrepreneur, encouraging the graduates to pursue their dreams relentlessly.
