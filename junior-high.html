Junior High School

Grades 7-10 curriculum aligned with the K-12 Basic Education Program. Students explore various subject areas while developing critical thinking, research skills, and character.

Core subjects include English, Mathematics, Science, Filipino, Araling Panlipunan, TLE, MAPEH, and Values Education. Students also participate in co-curricular activities.
